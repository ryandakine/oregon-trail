Mood Arc Color Script — 8 segments × 3 tone tiers

Phase D process artifact for docs/design/graphics-pop-research.md §3. The Firewatch method: settle the journey on one page before any shader moves.

Two places, and only two. Every hex on this page also lives in public/lib/palette.mjs, and nowhere else. The role each key plays in each segment lives in public/lib/segments.mjs, which stores key names, not colors. If you change a hex here, change it there — there is no third copy and no build step to catch a drifted one.
Reading a cell. Top to bottom is a landscape cross-section: zenith, horizon, far band, ground, ground-alt. The chip in the footer strip is that segment's single accent — the 10% of the 60/30/10 budget. Sky (zenith + horizon) is the 60%, terrain (ground + ground-alt) the 30%. far is the same terrain under atmospheric perspective: always cooler and lower-chroma than its own ground. The darks (outline, silhouetteNear) are shared by all eight and are not on this page — that shared dark anchor is what keeps this one game instead of eight.
Tier derivation. Low is the authored ramp. Medium and High are computed from it by the rules below, in this order per role — anchor lerp, then desaturate, then compress value. Order matters: desaturating after the compression would let a bright segment keep its value, and compressing before the lerp would let the anchor raise it back. This mirrors crush() in public/three/sky.mjs, restated in sRGB/HSL because a color script has to be legible; the 3D runtime does the same three steps in linear space with harder numbers because ACES tone-mapping sits downstream of it.
  • Medium — 6% toward #4a4436, S × 0.88, L × 0.96. AESTHETIC_SPEC §5 "desat 10%".
  • High base — lerp toward #232838, clamp saturation into a band, then compress value against a soft knee: L' = L <= knee ? L : knee + (L-knee)*(ceil-knee)/(1-knee). Per role — zenith mix .18 / S×.70 into 9–28% / knee .28 / ceil .56; horizon .16 / .70 / 9–28% / .26 / .50; far .20 / .62 / 7–24% / .24 / .42; ground .20 / .65 / 7–24% / .20 / .34; ground-alt .20 / .65 / 6–22% / .16 / .28. Light ceiling lands at 56% L, base saturation inside 15–25%.
  • Why a knee and not a clamp. First pass hard-capped L at the ceiling. Everything bright landed on exactly the ceiling, all eight zeniths came out the same gray-blue, and the tier read as one segment repeated — the uniformity failure the research names. The knee leaves darks untouched and compresses only the top, so a segment that was lighter than its neighbour stays lighter. Same reason saturation is clamped into a band rather than multiplied: a plain multiplier drives an already-dull base to flat gray and takes the hue with it.
  • High accent — the protected path: mix .10 only, S clamped into 55–88%, knee .30 / ceil .56. It is the one thing in a High frame allowed to be saturated, and it is what carries segment identity once the sky and ground are crushed (worst neighbouring-accent separation across the eight is ~16 ΔE OKLab, against ~2–6 for the crushed terrain bands).
  • High horizon — 10% of sicklyHorizon mixed back in after the crush, so desaturation cannot eat it (HIGH_SICKLY_MIX in sky.mjs is 0.13). Kept low on purpose: at 13% across all eight segments the sickly band starts becoming the accent, and then every horror frame is the same frame. Under High the segment accent is the one saturated note; the sickly band is a thin line, not a tier-wide color.
Where the mile boundaries come from. Not invented. The worker owns the trail in worker/src/historical-context.json — 16 segments, GameState.position.current_segment_id — and every boundary below lands on a datum from that file. Six of the eight fall on a server segment boundary; the two that don't (mile 460, mile 874) fall on a landmark mile_marker, lm_chimney_rock and lm_south_pass, because seg_04 has to carry two looks and because the white beat should start the moment you crest the pass, not 100 miles past it. segmentForMiles() uses the same at-exactly-the-end-mile-you-are-still-here rule as getSegmentForMile() in worker/src/context-loader.ts, and neighbours cross-fade over a 40-mile window centred on each boundary, so nothing pops.
